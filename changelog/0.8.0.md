# 0.8.0 · 2026-11-27

[返回更新日志](../CHANGELOG.md)

### 首页：类型、感受、排序

首页顶上多了「类型」「感受」「排序」三块面板，滑动或点按钮就能打开。每一项可以单独清掉，也可以一下全部重置。「感觉」改叫「感受」。选了一个类别、里面还没有东西时，页面会写出这个类别的名字。

开着首页的时候，有人上了新东西，它会自己出现，不用刷新。卡片的排列也按最近的商品重排过，相像的东西挨在一起。

### 已拾故事：整段改写

自己写的话现在可以整段改，不用一句一句改。改过的句子照样先复查，确认时看到哪一版，发出去的就是哪一版。

### 用邮箱注册也能收贺卡了

用邮箱和密码注册时，会收到一封验证信。设置页能看到邮箱验证了没有，可以重发验证信，也可以再查一次。验证过的邮箱就能收定时贺卡。领礼物时邮箱还没验证的，页面上直接有验证的按钮。

### 一件东西的几个款式，并成一张卡

同一件东西的不同尺寸或颜色，可以并成一张卡，在卡上切换。每一款还是自己的价格、库存和故事，加进 Picks 的是你选中的那一款。卖家在库存页勾选两件以上，就能「合并」「拆开」或「延伸」。

### 翻译又能用了

段落末尾的翻译前几天一直失败，现在又能用了。

### 还有这些

- 手机上打开首页，第二页不会再提前翻出来。
- 左栏折起时，鼠标停着不动它不会自己弹出来；弹出以后，鼠标移回左栏也不会闪一下就关上。
- 商品说明后面几行带「·」的句子，比如「规格：150 ml · 300 ml」，现在完整显示，不再被截掉。
- 帖子和商品页的转发按钮统一了。保存过的续写，保存的人登录后能再读到。
- 个人页的书架：重置以后马上显示默认书架；没读到的时候会直接说没读到，不会显示一片空白。

### Home: Type, Sense, Sort

The top of the home page has three new panels, Type, Sense and Sort. Swipe or tap a button to open them. Each choice can be cleared on its own, or all of them reset at once. Vibe is now called Sense. When a category you picked has nothing in it yet, the page names that category.

While the home page is open, something new that someone lists appears by itself, without a reload. The cards are also rearranged from the latest listings, so similar things sit side by side.

### Picked stories: editing the whole passage

You can now edit your own passage as a whole instead of one sentence at a time. Edited sentences are still checked first, and the version you saw when you confirmed is the one that is published.

### Email sign-ups can receive cards

Signing up with an email and password now sends a verification email. Settings shows whether your email is verified, and lets you send the email again or check again. Once your email is verified, you can receive scheduled cards. If you claim a gift before verifying, the page gives you the verify button right there.

### Several versions of one item, in one card

Different sizes or colours of the same item can be grouped into one card and switched on the card. Each version keeps its own price, stock and story, and the one you pick is the one added to Picks. On the inventory page, sellers who tick two or more items can Merge, Split or Extend them.

### Translation works again

The translate option at the end of a paragraph had been failing for a few days. It works again.

### Also

- On phones, the second page of the home page no longer flips open early.
- With the left rail folded, a mouse resting still no longer opens it; once it is open, moving back onto the rail no longer flashes it shut.
- Later lines of a description with a "·" in them, such as "Size: 150 ml · 300 ml", now show in full instead of being cut off.
- Share buttons on posts and product pages are now the same everywhere. A continuation you saved can be read again once you sign in.
- Shelves on your profile page: a reset shows the default shelf at once, and when the shelf can't be loaded the page says so instead of showing nothing.
