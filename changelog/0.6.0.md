### 发货邮件是一张卡片

卖家订好运单两小时后，你收到的邮件现在是一张横版卡片：正面是这件商品的图，背面写着你的名字和一句应季的祝福。点一下卡片就翻面；邮箱翻不了时，点卡片会打开它自己的网页。邮件里还写着快递取件后预计几个工作日送达，可以查看配送信息。按收货地的时间，晚上 9 点到早上 8 点之间该发的邮件，等到早上 8 点再发。

### 我的订单

「我的订单」里，有故事的包裹显示「收货拾故事」，代替「确认收货」和「查看详情」；点订单号进入详情。确认收货前会问一句「包裹真的到了吗？」。

### 左栏

每个页面打开时左栏都收起，页面居中；你在一页上展开或收起左栏，下一页照着来。好友列表收成一列头像，找人的 ＋ 就在旁边；「类别」筛选的图标换成和名字对应的样子。

### The dispatch email is a card

The email you get two hours after the seller books the label is now a landscape card: the item's picture on the front, and your name with a line for the season on the back. Tap the card to turn it over; where a mailbox cannot turn it, a tap opens the card's own page. The email also gives the expected delivery in business days after the carrier collects the parcel, with a link to the delivery details. An email due between 9 pm and 8 am at the delivery address waits until 8 am there.

### My orders

In My orders, a parcel with a story shows Receive it and tell its story in place of Mark received and View Details, and the order number opens the details. Before you mark a parcel received, you are asked "Has the parcel really arrived?".

### The left rail

Every page now opens with the rail folded and the page centred, and if you open or fold the rail on one page, the next page keeps it that way. The friends list is one column of faces, with the + to find someone beside it, and the icons in the Type filter now picture what their names say.
