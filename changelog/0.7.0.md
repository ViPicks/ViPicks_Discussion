# 0.7.0 · 2026-11-23

[返回更新日志](../CHANGELOG.md)

### 黑五

惟拾全店黑五：商品按标价打九折，到 11 月 30 日（悉尼时间），12 月 1 日起恢复标价。首页顶上的活动条、活动页、商品页、Picks 和结账页都换成黑底的 FRI 印，价格旁写「黑五价」。手机上，「黑五」两个字也是毛笔字。

### 定时贺卡

送礼页「一次性链接」旁边多了「定时贺卡」。写好贺卡，可以加封面，用邮箱找到收件人，再选一个悉尼时间，精确到分钟。到了那一分钟，贺卡自己送进对方的藏品。在「送出的礼物」里能看到每张卡是草稿、待寄出还是已寄出；还没寄出的，可以取消定时。

收件人要用 Google 或 Apple 登录过一次，邮箱才算确认过。只用邮箱和密码注册的账号，暂时收不到。

### 好友

加好友不用先互相关注了。你发申请时会关注对方，对方通过时会回关你。编辑资料时多了「谁能申请加我」：所有人、我关注的人，或者谁都不行。按「通过」之前，页面会先告诉你通过后你们会互相关注；你店里有互惠价的话，还会说明互惠价会对对方生效。左栏好友的头像外面多了一圈主题色的圆环。

### 已拾故事：自己写的句子先复查

你自己写的句子，比如改过的字、开头那一句，要先复查，通过了才公开。没复查的句子下面画着波浪线，可以按「再检查」再查一次。写到具体时间或地点的句子，改成宽泛的说法再复查。

确认时看到哪一版，发出去的就是哪一版，重试也不变。发布以后，点「看我的故事」会打开那条帖子。有一句在页面上显示成另一种语言时，改字也按那种语言来。

### 结账

结账页的汇总，每个数只出现一次。每个卖家那一块先写原价，再写活动减掉的，最后是互惠价或好友价减掉的。

### 还有这些

- 从「我的订单」点进订单，页面从顶上开始，不再停在页脚。
- 喜欢页上，书架里已经没有的卡片，点喜欢或取消没成功时会离开。
- 卖家打印收货标签时，「打印偏移」多了一项：顺时针转几度（±2° 以内），浏览器会记住。
- 已拾故事有三个选项换了更直白的说法，已经问出去的题不变。
- 卖家在左栏「更多」里把「库存」加进工具列表时，World、编辑精选和好友的帖子页上也会显示「✓」和「已在工具列表，本页隐藏」，不再像没按上。
- 手机 Safari 上，左栏的背景一直延伸到页面底部。

### Black Friday

Black Friday at The ViPicks store: 10% off store prices until 30 November, Sydney time, and store prices return on 1 December. The strip at the top of the home page, the campaign page, product pages, Picks and checkout carry a black FRI seal, and prices are labelled Black Friday price. On phones, the Chinese title 黑五 now shows in the brush script too.

### Scheduled cards

The gift page has a new Scheduled card, next to Gift link. Write the card, add a cover if you like, find the recipient by email, and pick a Sydney time, to the minute. At that minute the card lands in their Collections by itself. Gifts I sent shows whether each card is a draft, to be sent, or sent, and you can cancel the schedule of a card that has not gone yet.

The recipient needs to have signed in with Google or Apple once, which confirms their email. Accounts registered with only an email and password cannot receive cards yet.

### Friends

You no longer need to follow each other before adding a friend. Sending a request follows them, and accepting follows you back. Editing your profile has a new setting, Who can send you friend requests: Anyone, People you follow, or No one. Before you accept, the page tells you that you'll follow each other, and, if your store has a Mutual Price, that it will apply to them. Friends' avatars in the left rail now have a ring in the theme colour.

### Picked stories: your own sentences are checked first

Sentences you write yourself, such as edited words and the opening line, are checked first and go public once they pass. A sentence not checked yet has a wavy underline, and Check again checks it once more. A sentence that names an exact time or place needs to be made broader and checked again.

The version you saw when you confirmed is the version that is published, retries included. After publishing, See my story opens the post. When a sentence shows in the other language on the page, editing it uses that language too.

### Checkout

The checkout summary shows each figure once. Each seller's block lists the original price, then what a promotion takes off, then what a Mutual Price or friend price takes off.

### Also

- Opening an order from My orders starts at the top of the page, not at the footer.
- On the liked page, a card that is no longer on the shelf now leaves when your like or unlike on it is refused.
- When sellers print receive labels, Printer offset has one more setting: a clockwise turn in degrees, within ±2°, remembered by the browser.
- Three answer options in picked stories are worded more plainly; questions already asked keep their wording.
- When sellers add Inventory to their tools from More in the left rail, World, editorial and friends' post pages now show a ✓ and In your tools, hidden on this page, instead of seeming to do nothing.
- In Safari on phones, the left rail's background now runs to the bottom of the page.
