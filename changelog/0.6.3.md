# 0.6.3 · 2026-11-16

[返回更新日志](../CHANGELOG.md)

### 发货卡片

卡片两面都是同样的横版比例，四周留一圈一样宽的边，商品图放在中间的窗口里。

### 还有这些

- 结账出错时，提示只用你选的语言，不再夹着英文。
- 卖家上架时挑选 3D 素材，商品名跟着你选的语言。
- 在 iPhone 和 iPad 上，帖子页的「在 App 里打开」不再压住「查看商品」。

### The dispatch card

Both sides of the card now have the same landscape proportions, with an even border all round, and the item's picture sits in a window in the middle.

### Also

- When checkout fails, the message is in your language only, with no English under it.
- When a seller picks a 3-D model for a listing, product names follow the language they choose.
- On iPhone and iPad, a post page's Open in the app link no longer covers View Product.
