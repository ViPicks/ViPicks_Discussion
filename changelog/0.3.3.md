# 0.3.3 · 2026-11-08

[返回更新日志](../CHANGELOG.md)

### 喜欢的帖子

「喜欢」页里的帖子改成和首页一样的方块卡片，只显示商品的抠图。商品、图片和价格都和首页一致；商品下架的帖子不再出现在这里。

### 账号设置

设置页最下面「更多设置即将推出」那张卡去掉了，最后一项是「关于惟拾」。

### 好友自提

互相关注的两个人之间买卖，结账时可以选「好友自提」：没有运费和服务费，东西由你们自己约时间交接，买家和卖家各点一次「已交接」就完成。卖家要先在商品的配送方式里勾选好友自提。ViPicks 只提供这个方式，不替好友之间的交易做仲裁。

### 收礼

收下视频礼物，或者第一次为实物礼物填写收货地址时，你会同时关注送礼人。按下之前，按钮上会先写明。

### Liked posts

Posts on the Liked page now use the same square cards as the home page, showing only the product's cut-out. Product, picture and price match the home page, and a post whose listing has ended no longer appears there.

### Account settings

The "More settings coming soon" card at the bottom of Settings is gone. The last item is About ViPicks.

### Friend pickup

When two people who follow each other buy and sell, checkout offers Friend pickup: no postage and no service fee, and the two of you arrange the handover yourselves. It is complete once the buyer and the seller have each pressed Handed over. The seller ticks Friend pickup in the listing's delivery options first. ViPicks provides the way to share; it does not arbitrate trades between friends.

### Receiving gifts

Keeping a video gift, or giving the first delivery address for a real-item gift, now also follows the person who sent it. The button says so before you press it.
