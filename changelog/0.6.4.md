### 已拾故事：六问、生成、发布

已拾故事现在问六个问题，不再是九个：物的一生、人与物、卖家，三组各两题。答完最后一题，页面会把你的回答写成一段第一人称的故事。不满意可以再生成，每个故事一共三次，也可以自己改字；你自己写的字不会被改掉。故事里具体的时间和地点会写得笼统一些。

包裹到了以后，点「确认收货并发布」，故事就成了一条帖子。

### 收货标签

卖家可以在发货页或已售列表里打印收货标签，上面有一个二维码。买家扫一下，就到自己订单里的那个包裹。

### 还有这些

- 一段时间没人结账之后，第一次结账也能算出运费。
- 订单会显示揽收日期和物流状态，包裹签收以后就能确认收货。
- 新账号的语言，跟着你登录时页面的语言。

### Picked stories: six questions, written for you, published

A picked story now asks six questions instead of nine: two each about the object's life, you and the object, and the seller. After the last answer, the page writes your answers into a short first-person story. You can generate it again, three times per story in all, or edit the words yourself; anything you wrote yourself is never rewritten. Exact times and places in the story are kept general.

When the parcel arrives, Confirm and publish turns the story into a post.

### Receive labels

Sellers can print a receive label, with a QR code on it, from the shipping page or the sold list. The buyer scans it and lands on that parcel in their own order.

### Also

- The first checkout after a quiet spell now prices shipping too.
- Orders show the pickup date and tracking status, and you can confirm receipt once the parcel is delivered.
- A new account's language follows the language of the page you signed in on.
- The liked page keeps its shelf as you left it, and hearts you tapped stay after the shelf refreshes.
