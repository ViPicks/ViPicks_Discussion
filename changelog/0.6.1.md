# 0.6.1 · 2026-11-16

[返回更新日志](../CHANGELOG.md)

### 商品名跟着你的语言

订单列表、订单页、下单成功页和订单确认邮件里，商品有中英文两个名字时，按你选的语言显示。

### 下单成功和订单邮件

下单成功页不再盖「成」字章，订单邮件标题旁的印章也去掉了。确认邮件不再提故事，故事的入口随发货邮件一起来。

### Product names in your language

In the orders list, the order page, the Order Confirmed page and the confirmation email, a product with both a Chinese and an English name now shows the one in your language.

### Order Confirmed and order emails

The Order Confirmed page no longer carries the 成 seal, and the order emails no longer have a seal beside their titles. The confirmation email no longer mentions a story; the way into the story comes with the dispatch email.
