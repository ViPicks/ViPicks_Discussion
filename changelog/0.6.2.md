# 0.6.2 · 2026-11-16

### 结账和订单页

结账、下单成功、物流跟踪和卖家发货页上的字，现在都跟着你选的语言。付款后如果出了问题，提示会说明钱是否已经付出，拿不准时请先看订单，免得重复付款。

### 查看物流

「查看物流」直接打开承运快递公司自己的查询页；卖家订好运单后，页面写的是实际承运的快递公司。

### 发货卡片

卡片正面就是商品图，按图本来的比例铺满，上下不再留白。

### Checkout and order pages

Checkout, the Order Confirmed page, tracking and the seller's shipping page now follow the language you choose. If something goes wrong after you pay, the message says whether the money has gone through, and to check your orders when that is unclear, so you do not pay twice.

### Tracking

Track shipment now opens the carrier's own tracking page, and once the seller books a label, the page names the carrier that takes the parcel.

### The dispatch card

The front of the card is now the picture itself, full width and in its own proportions, with no band above or below.
